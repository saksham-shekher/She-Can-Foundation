Our Story

Global Vision, Local Action

She Can Foundation is an NGO registered under the Indian Society Act, 1860, dedicated to empowering women and creating a more equitable society. The foundation provides support, resources, and training to help women from marginalized communities overcome barriers and reach their full potential.

Through menstrual hygiene support, awareness workshops, dignity kits, education, training, and advocacy, She Can Foundation works with communities to promote gender equality, healthcare access, education, and economic growth.

Guided by the belief that every woman deserves the opportunity to thrive, She Can Foundation invites people to contribute money, skills, or time to build lasting change.

What We Do

Our Empowerment Approach

Programs focused on dignity, education, training, advocacy, and sustainable community support.

🩸

Menstrual Hygiene Support

Providing free sanitary pads, dignity kits, and menstrual hygiene support so girls can continue school with confidence.

📚

Education & Healthcare Awareness

Raising awareness on women’s issues, health, education, and rights through community workshops and campaigns.

💼

Training & Skill Development

Offering resources, training, and mentorship to help women build confidence, skills, and economic independence.

🤲

Community Outreach

Working with local communities, organizations, and supporters to create effective and sustainable impact.
